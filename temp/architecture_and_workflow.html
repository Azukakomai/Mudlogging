Ingestion Layer (parser.py)
Handles dirty rig metadata headers, multi-format parsing (.las, .csv, .txt, .xlsx), automated channel aliases, and float conversions in volatile RAM via io.StringIO.
Logic Layer (engine.py)
Vectorized in-memory DAG computing 16 ratios and majority-vote fluid classification (Gas, Oil, Water, No Show) with 100% mathematical auditability (SPE PRMS Ch. 4).
Presentation Layer (app.py)
Interactive dashboard rendering 21 dedicated tracks, synchronized depth crosshairs, KPI metrics, and dynamic Column & Formula Manager popups with live preview.
Evaluator Layer (evaluator.py)
Executes automated tensor verification, ground-truth benchmarking vs Wireline Formation Testers (RFT/MDT), Confusion Matrix, and Macro-$F_1$ scoring.

Step 1 – 2 Ingestion & In-Memory Sanitization
User drags and drops raw field logs. Parser cleans metadata, normalizes channel aliases, and builds numeric buffers in volatile RAM in less than 0.02s.
Step 3 – 4 16 Deterministic Ratio Derivations
Vectorized calculation of Pixler ratios, Haworth parameters, Dryness, Carbon Index, GOW, WBS, and GOR without black-box ML risks.
Step 5 – 7 Multi-Criteria Expert Classification
The majority-vote rule matrix tallies 8 independent indicators per depth step to classify reservoir fluid regimes with full SPE PRMS audit traceability.
Step 8 – 11 Interactive Exploration & Export
Synchronized depth crosshair tracking across 21 tracks, live formula editing with real-time audit preview, and 1-click deliverable exports.

\begin{abstracteng}
In petroleum exploration and drilling operations, subsurface fluid identification has traditionally relied on post-drilling wireline logging (WL) or logging-while-drilling (LWD) tools, which introduces substantial operational costs, rig standby time, and borehole risks. While Gas While Drilling (GWD) chromatographic data ($C_1$ through $C_5$) is acquired continuously during the first drilling run as the drill bit crushes rock formations, its diagnostic potential remains severely underutilized. Currently available formation evaluation software suites function primarily as passive data plotters that merely graph log curves against depth, lacking integrated, automated pipelines to transform raw gas fractions into actionable hydrocarbon fluid forecasts. To address this operational gap, this research develops a dedicated, open-source web-based decision support system for automated multi-ratio petrophysical analysis and deterministic hydrocarbon fluid classification. The proposed software automatically ingests raw mud logs, derives 16+ petrophysical gas indicators (including expanded Haworth show ratios, classic Pixler ratios, and composite fluid indices), and executes deterministic decision-tree logic to forecast fluid regimes (Gas Zone, Oil Zone, or Water/Non-Productive Sand) directly from first-drilling GWD data in seconds. The application provides an interactive 21-track continuous depth visualizer alongside dynamic column and formula managers. By providing reliable, transparent, and immediate fluid forecasting on the initial drilling pass, this system reduces dependence on expensive wireline logging verification, mitigates capital risk, and democratizes advanced petrophysical analytics.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} \textit{Gas While Drilling (GWD), Hydrocarbon Forecasting, Deterministic Classification, Data Visualization, Decision Support System, Well Logging.}
\end{abstracteng}
